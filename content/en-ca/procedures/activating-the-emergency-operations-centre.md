> **Golden rule:** when in doubt, activate. Standing down an unneeded team costs an hour. Activating too late costs far more.

## When to activate

- A power outage longer than 4 hours, or announced as long
- A winter storm, freezing rain or high wind warning
- Phone or Internet service lost at more than one municipal site
- Any request from emergency services or from general management

## In the first 15 minutes

- Call general management at 450 555-0102, or the alternate
- Call each mission lead in the order shown on the [Contacts](/en-ca/contacts/) page
- If a call fails after two tries, move to the alternate and note the time
- Set the time and place of the first meeting, the town hall by default
- Open the operations log, on paper or digital, and record the activation time

## In the first hour

- Hold the first meeting: situation, affected sites, vulnerable people, immediate needs
- Give each mission to a named lead, and record it in the log
- Decide the first message to residents, see [Communicating without a network](/en-ca/procedures/communicating-without-a-network/)
- Check that the town hall generator is ready to start
- Set the next meeting, no later than two hours away

## If phone and Internet are down

- Every team member goes to the town hall at the next full hour
- The first to arrive opens the room, turns on the radio and opens the log
- Missing members are reached by runner, critical missions first

## Ending the alert

- General management declares the end of the alert and records it in the log
- Tell residents the alert is over, using the same means as the alert
- Plan a lessons-learned review within 14 days, and revise this procedure
