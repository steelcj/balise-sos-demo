> **Message to residents:** bring water to a rolling boil for at least one minute before drinking it, cooking with it, preparing baby bottles or brushing teeth.

## When to issue the advisory

- A major break or a large pressure drop in the water system
- A treatment plant outage longer than 2 hours
- An abnormal test result, or a request from public health authorities

## Issuing the advisory

- The drinking water lead confirms the affected area and the time
- Tell general management, and the emergency operations centre if it is active
- Spread the message above by every means available: website, posters, door to door
- Go door to door first to daycares, seniors' residences and food businesses
- Post the advisory at the town hall, the corner store and the community centre

## During the advisory

- Have bottled water at the community centre for people who cannot boil water because they have no power
- Track samples and test results, and record them in the log

## Lifting the advisory

- Lift the advisory only when test results allow it
- Announce the lifting by the same means, with advice to run the taps for a few minutes
- Take down every poster
