> **The principle:** a known place, a known time. Everyone already knows where to go and when, without being told.

## Within the team

- Rally point: the town hall, at every full hour, for as long as the alert lasts
- Portable radios from the municipal garage: channel 1 for the operations centre, channel 2 for field crews
- Every radio message starts with who is calling and who is called, and ends with "over"
- One runner per site, on foot or by vehicle, for written messages
- Every important message is written, dated, signed, and copied into the log

## To residents

- Posters at the three sites and the corner store, updated at least twice a day, showing the time of update
- Door to door for vulnerable residents, see [Extended power outage](/en-ca/procedures/extended-power-outage/)
- As soon as a network returns, publish the same message on the website, social media and the municipal answering machine

## Message template

- What is happening, in one sentence
- What the municipality is doing
- What residents need to do
- Where to find help: community centre, 3, place de l'Église
- Time of the next message

## When the network returns

- Update this copy of Balise on every device, see [Balise](/en-ca/balise/)
- Check that every written message has been copied into the log
