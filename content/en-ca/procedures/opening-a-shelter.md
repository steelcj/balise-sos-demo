## Deciding to open

The emergency operations centre decides to open when residents can no longer stay safely at home: cold, flooding, evacuation. For a short outage, a daytime **warming centre** may be enough.

## Opening the centre

- Start the community centre generator and check the heating
- Set up reception at the entrance: table, paper register, pens, a sign reading "Accueil / Welcome"
- Set up a charging corner: power bars on the generator, one outlet per person at a time
- Prepare a quiet area for older people and families with young children
- Announce the opening, see [Communicating without a network](/en-ca/procedures/communicating-without-a-network/)

## Welcoming each person

- Record in the register the name, address, number of people and a way to reach a relative
- Ask about particular needs: medication, mobility, a pet, diet
- Pets stay in the room set aside for them, caged or leashed
- Never turn away a person in distress; if the centre is full, tell the emergency operations centre

## While open

- One lead on site at all times, with a radio
- Count the people present at each shift change and record it in the register
- Report anyone sick or injured to the emergency operations centre; in an emergency, call 911

## Closing the centre

- Close only when every person has a safe place to go
- Hand the paper register to general management, who keeps it confidential
- Clean up, restock and note what ran short
