## 24 hours ahead, as soon as the warning is issued

- Fuel every snow-clearing vehicle and every generator
- Check salt and abrasive stocks
- Charge the portable radios, lights and backup batteries
- Print the [printable version](/en-ca/print/) if the emergency binder is more than a month old
- Tell residents what is planned and that street parking is banned

## During the storm

- Clear in this order: access to the community centre, rue Principale, chemin du Lac, then the rest of the network
- No crew works alone; each crew has a radio and reports its position every hour
- Rotate operators at least every 12 hours
- If ice brings down trees or wires, block the area and call 911; never touch a fallen wire

## If the power fails

- Follow [Extended power outage](/en-ca/procedures/extended-power-outage/) at the same time

## After the storm

- Walk around every municipal building: roofs, entrances, emergency exits
- List the damage and record it in the log, with photos if possible
- Put equipment back in order and restock
