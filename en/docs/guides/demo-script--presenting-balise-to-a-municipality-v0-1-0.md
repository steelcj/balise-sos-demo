# Demo Script: Presenting Balise to a Municipality

Version: 0.1.0
Status: Draft
Style Guide: style-guide--plain-language-for-general-audiences

## Abstract

This is a script for showing Balise to a municipality in about fifteen minutes. It lists what to prepare, the moments that make the idea land, the questions people usually ask, and how to close with a next step.

## Prepare

- A laptop with the downloaded copy unzipped on the desktop, and the web copy opened once so it is stored
- A phone with the web copy added to the home screen
- A USB key with the zip, to leave behind
- A printed binder from the "Printable version" page
- Know where the laptop's Wi-Fi switch is

## The fifteen minutes

**The problem, in their words (2 minutes).** Ask about the last big storm. What failed first? Where were the procedures? Who could reach them? Let them tell it; it is usually the Internet, then the sign-in, then the phone.

**Pull the plug (3 minutes).** Turn off Wi-Fi on the laptop and put the phone in airplane mode, in front of them. Open Balise on both. It works. This is the moment the idea lands, so let it sit.

**Find something under stress (3 minutes).** Ask someone to find what to do if the generator has to start. Type "generatrice" without the accent. Open the procedure, tick a few boxes. Point at who is responsible and when it was last reviewed.

**Switch language (1 minute).** Press "English" on a procedure. It lands on the same procedure, not the home page.

**The three copies (3 minutes).** Show the footer line: which kind of copy, how old. Show the "This copy" page and the download. Hand over the printed binder: the copy that works when every battery is flat.

**What it would take (3 minutes).** Their procedures in markdown, two languages, a review date and an owner for each. A web address, theirs or ours. One afternoon of setup on their computers. No accounts, no licences, no vendor to depend on; every file is theirs.

## Questions people ask

**What if we already have a plan in Word?** It becomes the content. The steps get shorter and get boxes; the plan itself stays theirs.

**Who updates it?** Whoever owns the plan today. Editing is a text file; a web editing screen can be added.

**Is our data on the Internet?** Only what they choose to publish. The list of vulnerable residents stays on paper, under lock, as the procedures themselves say.

**What does it cost to run?** Hosting a static site is free on GitHub Pages or nearly free elsewhere; it can also be hosted on their own server.

## Close

Offer to convert one real procedure, their choice, into Balise, in both languages, for the next meeting. Leave the USB key and the binder.
